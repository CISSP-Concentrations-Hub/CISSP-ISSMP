## Key Domains of the ISSMP Certification  
The ISSMP exam covers six critical domains:  

### 1. Leadership and Business Management (20%)  
- Define security’s role in organizational culture.  
- Align security programs with business goals.  

### 2. Systems Lifecycle Management (18%)  
- Manage security integration into SDLC.  
- Oversee vulnerability and change management.  

### 3. Risk Management (19%)  
- Conduct organizational risk assessments.  
- Manage supply chain security risks.  

### 4. Threat Intelligence and Incident Management (17%)  
- Establish threat intelligence programs.  
- Develop incident handling processes.  

### 5. Contingency Management (15%)  
- Develop and maintain disaster recovery plans.  
- Facilitate business continuity strategies.  

### 6. Law, Ethics, and Security Compliance Management (11%)  
- Manage compliance with laws and regulations.  
- Oversee internal and external audit processes.
